## Planning workload
100 staff × 2 distinct tasks/day × 30 days = 6,000 submissions/month. At 2 compressed photos × 0.5MB = **6GB new evidence/month**, or 24GB if each reaches 2MB. Two authenticated views/photo gives roughly 12GB or 48GB/month of evidence download, plus draft upload duplication and optional backups. Client drafts and canonical evidence both remain until an approved cleanup policy exists: storage may grow roughly **12–48GB/month**, excluding resubmission revisions. No automatic retention in delivered code means accumulation continues, not a stable monthly plateau.

100 staff × 4 dashboard loads/day × ~25 eligible tasks ≈ 10,000 task document reads/day after assignment-query optimization, plus profiles, submissions, quota transactions, history, managers, rule profile reads and retries. ~200 submissions + 200 reviews/day produce a few thousand writes/day including ledger/audit/history/quota. Large task libraries or manager refresh frequency increase reads materially. App Check/Functions deny abuse only within their respective controls; no estimate is a hard cap.

Budgetary allowance for this small workload: **US$5–30/month initially**, excluding tax, Google Workspace licensing, paid backup/PITR, extended logs, large retention and heavy egress. This is an engineering allowance, not a calculated bill; free US-region allowances may reduce costs, while cross-region transfer, high image size or abuse may exceed it. Model actual costs as: charged reads×regional rate + charged writes×rate + retained GiB-month×storage rate + downloads GiB×egress rate + operations + Functions GB-seconds/vCPU/invocations + Hosting + Artifact Registry + optional backups/Drive. Measure one staging week before approving a production budget. Set reviewed budget alerts and Storage growth alerts; alerts do not automatically stop spend.
